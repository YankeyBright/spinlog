# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, accept constructive technical feedback, focus on the work, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban contributors when behavior threatens a safe and productive project environment. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as practical.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
